Syringe Appearance

This guide illustrates syringe styles associated with Bluerexall product packaging. The examples show differences in cap appearance and printed markings; they do not specify the contents of every product or packaging series.

Understanding Visual Differences

Cap Appearance

Cap color, shape, or style may differ between packaging series. A clear, black, or gray cap alone does not determine authenticity.

Printed Markings

The presence, layout, or appearance of graduation markings may vary. The illustration should not be used to infer capacity, dosing, or technical specifications.

Packaging Series

Visual details may change between packaging series. Compare the information supplied with your specific product and use the official verification route if you have questions.

Appearance alone is not an authenticity check. Differences in caps, markings, or packaging do not, by themselves, establish that a product is authentic or counterfeit.
